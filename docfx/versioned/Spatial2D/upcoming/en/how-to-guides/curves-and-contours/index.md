# Curves and Contours

Practical guides for constructing and querying curves and closed boundaries:

- [Project a point onto a curve](project-a-point-onto-a-curve.md) to find the closest boundary
  point and distance.
- [Find curve intersections](find-curve-intersections.md) by casting a directed ray.
- [Build a closed contour](build-a-closed-contour.md) from vertices, segments, arcs, Bezier, or
  spline paths.
- [Create a B-spline](create-a-b-spline.md) from a degree, control points, and a knot vector.
- [Create a NURBS curve](create-a-nurbs-curve.md) when rational weights must influence the shape.

For the underlying geometry model, see [Curves](../../concepts/geometry-model/curves.md) and
[Contours](../../concepts/geometry-model/contours.md).
